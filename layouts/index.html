Disney's Fort Wilderness Resort & Campground

Measured, not repeated.

You don't choose your campsite at Fort Wilderness — you book a category, request a loop, and a cast member assigns you a number at check-in. This is the reference for what that number actually gets you.

Every figure is measured from public aerial imagery, comes from Disney's own published specs, or is credited to whoever published it first until we verify it ourselves. Where something hasn't been measured, the page says so rather than quietly leaving it out.

About Fort Wilderness

Fort Wilderness sits on 750 acres of pine and cypress forest in the Magic Kingdom resort area — Disney's only campground, and the only Walt Disney World resort where you bring your own roof. Deer, rabbits, ducks and armadillos live on the property. There are no cars to Magic Kingdom: you get there by boat, and everywhere else by bus.

Campsites~845 across 21 loops, in five categories
Standard on every siteWater, electric, cable television, picnic table, charcoal grill
SewerAll categories except Tent / Pop-Up
Maximum occupancy8 guests, for arrivals from 1 January 2026
RigsTents through 45 ft-plus motorhomes, depending on category
SwimmingMeadow Swimmin' Pool with a corkscrew waterslide, and the quieter Wilderness Swimmin' Pool
Also on propertyArchery, horseback riding, canoeing, woodland trails, golf cart rental, and an off-leash dog park

“Camp amid the magic of the great outdoors.”

Disney's own description of the resort — disneyworld.disney.go.com

Resort facts above are Disney's published information, linked so you can check them. Everything on a site page is measured or attributed separately — Disney publishes nothing about individual campsites, which is the reason this exists.
